Privacy policy

TodeMail is a macOS email app developed by Ryan Vong for private, personal use. This policy covers the app and this information website.

Google account access

TodeMail uses Google OAuth to connect accounts with your consent. It requests the gmail.modify scope to read email, search and cache messages, manage labels and read/flag/folder state, synchronize drafts, and send mail when you use those features. This scope does not grant immediate permanent deletion of messages bypassing Trash.

The app processes account addresses, message and thread identifiers, headers, recipients, subjects, bodies, labels and attachments as needed for its email features. Google account passwords are not collected by TodeMail; authorization takes place with Google.

Use and local storage

Mail data is used for displaying, searching, composing, synchronizing and managing your mail, plus optional features you enable. Downloaded mail, indexes, drafts and related settings are stored locally on your Mac. Authentication credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. The app does not require a developer-operated mail-storage server.

Local caches and backups can contain readable private mail. Local storage is not a promise of app-level encryption or protection from someone who controls your Mac. Optional backups and exports write data to the location you choose; a location synchronized by another service is subject to that service’s practices.

Optional AI and connected clients

Ordinary mail features do not require AI. If you use selected-context Codex features, the reviewed email content or composition context and your instructions can be sent to OpenAI through the installed ChatGPT-connected Codex CLI. Query-only interpretation can send the typed search sentence and date/time-zone context without adding mail content.

Local MCP access is a separate opt-in. Enabling it for selected accounts allows connected local AI clients to search and read cached mail from those accounts without a TodeMail review of each individual request. Those clients may transmit the returned data to their model providers. Disable MCP or revoke account grants to stop future access; data already shared cannot be recalled.

Cloud AI processing is not entirely on-device. Use of shared data is also governed by the connected service’s terms, privacy policy and your account settings. TodeMail does not promise that a third-party service has zero retention or excludes model training for every account.

Other network requests and sharing

The app communicates with connected email providers to perform mail operations. External images are blocked by default; loading them can disclose your IP address and the image request to the image host. Opening links or using unsubscribe actions can contact outside services. Exporting mail, sending email and granting AI-client access share data with the destinations you choose.

The developer does not operate an advertising or data-resale service for your mail. Google user data is not used by TodeMail to serve advertisements or build advertising profiles.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Local mail archives and drafts can remain after disconnecting an account. Disconnecting is not equivalent to deleting downloaded data. You can revoke Google access through your Google account connections. To remove local data, remove the corresponding app data and any backups or exports you created; contact the developer for guidance before deleting files. Removing the app alone may leave app data and Keychain entries behind.

Revoking access stops future authorized requests once Google enforces the revocation. It does not erase copies already saved locally, sent to recipients, exported or shared with a connected AI provider.

Google API data policy

TodeMail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This statement is not a claim of Google verification or endorsement.

This website

This static website has no app login, mailbox access, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by TodeMail. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Public GitHub issues are visible to others.

Contact and changes

For app or privacy questions, contact Ryan Vong through the TodeMail information repository. Do not include private messages, credentials, tokens or security codes. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above identifies this version.
