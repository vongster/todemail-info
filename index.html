TodeMail is a native email app for reading, searching, writing and managing mail across connected accounts.

Built for private, personal use. This website provides app and privacy information; it does not offer a public download or access to anyone’s mailbox.

Read the privacy policy ↗

01 / CONNECT

Google authorization

Gmail connections use Google’s browser-based consent flow. TodeMail requests Gmail access to read mail, manage labels and message state, and create drafts and send messages when you use those features.

02 / KEEP

A local mail archive

Downloaded mail and search indexes are stored on your Mac. Credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Cached history may be incomplete until synchronization finishes.

03 / CHOOSE

Optional AI tools

AI features are optional. Reviewed context can be sent to OpenAI through Codex. Separately enabled MCP access can share cached mail from chosen accounts with a connected AI client and its provider.

Private app. Clear information.

TodeMail is developed and maintained by Ryan Vong. It is independent of Google and OpenAI. The app’s OAuth publishing status is separate from whether the app is offered for public download.

For app or privacy questions, open an issue. Do not post private email, credentials or authorization codes.
